## Context

The issue was opened as a 2010+ categorisation complaint. The cause is `rule_short_garbage` (gate 6 in `determine_category`, `text_util.py:1337-1345`), which returns `Trash` unconditionally for any 1–3 token line without Czech diacritics, weak language ID and any weirdness. It is the largest single Trash producer — 6,333 firings, 28.4% of all Trash lines — and it fires hardest on post-2000 documents because those are tabular, one value per cell.

@david-spacil proposes gating it behind `_has_strong_garbage_evidence()`, the same predicate six section-9 rules use. Measured on both collections recomputed twice on `9f7559c`, the patch moves **367,208 lines and demotes none** — Trash→Clear 153,353, Noisy→Clear 119,956, Trash→Noisy 93,899 — dropping `Trash` by 247,252 lines (−8.68%) across 113,101 documents and 71,815,774 lines, with `Non-text` and `Empty` untouched. 484 of the promoted lines scored blind by a human annotator (0 = right, 1 = borderline, 2 = wrong; key held aside on shuffled samples), design-weighted with decade strata, give **76.5% right / 9.6% borderline / 13.9% wrong**, a 5.5 : 1 ratio — so roughly 26,000 garbage lines reach `Clear`, the top category rather than a reviewable middle state. `tools/SWEEP_NOTES.md` classifies the rule `LOAD-BEARING`. The earlier 378,735 figure was measured before `rule_domain_notation` landed; notation is now lifted without the patch, removing 11,532 lines (3.0%) from the population it acts on.

Two framings were tried and abandoned before the current one. The **symmetry argument** (that the rule is the seventh sibling of six gated rules) does not hold: `50472fb` added `qs < thresh_trash` to sites that were already gated and never touched this rule, which sits among eight *early-exit* rules that all return unconditionally. And **"367,208 lifted / 0 demoted"** is a theorem rather than a measurement — deleting an unconditional `return Trash` is monotone by construction — so the only evidence about whether the change is *right* is the 484-line grading.

## Research

**1. What separates the two populations at 1–2 tokens.**
*Superseded in part by §5.* Of the signals the pipeline **passes to `determine_category`**, nothing separates them: `vansasaasasa` and `malakofauna` agree on valid-word ratio (1.000), weird ratio (0.350), garbage density (0.000), gibberish (0), ghost share (0) and raw quality score (0.647 vs 0.647). A quality-score gate was built and measured: dead, because raw pre-alignment scores overlap completely (garbage 0.52–0.65, notation 0.49–0.86, with `II/C` and `I-VIII-c` *below* most garbage). §5 shows a signal the pipeline **computes but does not act on** does separate much of it.

**2. Whether perplexity can arbitrate.**
No, and it runs backwards. Both surviving routes gate on *low* language-ID confidence; FastText scores `oueussd` at 0.9163 and `malakofauna` at 0.56. With the cap lifted, real notation demotes at perplexity 3,000 while `oueussd` survives to 30,000. @david-spacil's recomputed values corroborate: `II/C` ≈ 6·10⁷ (correct) against `oueussd` ≈ 4,600 (garbage). Independently: because `SHORT_PPL_CAP` (850) < `HARD_SWEEP_PPL_MIN` (1,000) < `PPL_EXTREME_MIN` (3,000) < `PPL_GARBAGE_ABSOLUTE` (30,000), **no perplexity rule can fire at all on a 1–2 token line**.

**3. Whether the existing structural protections could be widened.**
`is_structured_line()` dispatches to seven predicates; all seven returned `False` on all 35 thread examples (245 evaluations, zero matches). But widening *that* predicate is the wrong lever — it is read at eleven places and is an absolute veto at the first statement of `_has_strong_garbage_evidence()`, so widening it would also disable `rule_allcaps`, `rule_garbage_density`, `rule_zero_alpha`, `rule_vowelless` and `rule_mid_uppercase`. Hence a separate, narrower predicate.

**4. Whether the wc/char boundary can isolate the problem lines.**
No. @david-spacil's error rates are flat — 31% / 28% / 25% by token count, 17–42% by character band with no monotone trend — and the tightest `wc <= 3, chars <= 24` box encloses 482 of 508 lines, 343 of them correct. `sektlll` and `edelite` (wrong) sit in the same band as `Kaaden` and `Pinii` (right).

**5. 🆕 The pipeline already computes a discriminating signal, and the short-line path throws it away.**
This is the finding that changes the plan, and it falsifies — for most of the class — the claim carried by the previous revision of this document, by `tests/test_text_utils.py:175` ("Separating `malakofauna` from `oueussd` needs a lexicon; no regex does"), by `text_util.py:1985` (`is_domain_notation`'s own docstring: "those from `oueussd` needs a lexicon rather than a pattern"), and by PR #48's *Known limitation* section.

`detect_fused_words()` (`text_util.py:757`), built on `_RE_FUSED_VOWEL_RUN` (`text_util.py:754`, 3+ vowel run) and `_RE_FUSED_CONSONANT_RUN` (`text_util.py:753`, 5+ consonant run), returns **1** for `oueussd`, `sektlll`, `cuxoaid`, `Tthts I` and **0** for `malakofauna`, `diapozitiv`, `Occipitale`, `Equus caballus`, `Kaaden`, `Pinii`, `kontext`, `Uniocrassus`, `Ossa tarsi`, `radius prox.sin.`, `1 ks`, `II/C`.

Section 7 (`rule_short_line`) **already reads it**, inside its `damage` term:

```python
or ((gibberish_present or detect_fused_words(text_source) > 0) and not structurally_clean)
```

with `structurally_clean = valid_word_ratio >= 1.0`. But `valid_word_ratio` is a **shape-only** heuristic — `compute_valid_ratio()` (`text_util.py:2253`) counts a token valid on length ≥ 3, ≥ 70% alphabetic, no strange character, no mid-word uppercase — so it is **1.0 for every line in this population**, `oueussd` included. The one witness that discriminates is suppressed by the one signal that cannot. Its own `len(core) > 14` clause is a separate false positive (`Skelettmaterial`), which is why the **run-only** form is the part worth reusing.

**6. 🆕 On this population the PR's gate is equivalent to deleting the rule.**
`_has_strong_garbage_evidence()` (`text_util.py:1646-1678`) returned **False on 16 of 16** disputed lines. With `valid_word_ratio` 1.0, `weird_ratio ≈ 0.35`, gibberish 0 and density 0.0, its only live clause is `lang_score <= 0.20 and orig_lang_score <= 0.50` — i.e. `orig_lang <= 0.40` after `TRUST_TIER_UNKNOWN` (0.50) scales it; the last conjunct misses by 0.05 (`weird_ratio >= 0.40` against 0.35). `thresh_trash = CATEG_TRASH_SCORE_MAX + 0.35 = 0.90` (`text_util.py:1434`) is nearly always true, so the predicate is the whole gate. Measured through the real `determine_category`:

| variant                        | real vocabulary → `Trash`     | garbage → not-`Trash`                    |
|--------------------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| baseline (`test` @ `4139e93`)  | 8 / 9 — the issue's complaint | 0 / 7                                    |
| + PR #48's gate                | 0 / 9                         | **7 / 7, all reaching `Clear`**          |
| run-only witness (no `len>14`) | —                             | flags 4 / 7, **0 / 9 false positives**   |
| full witness cascade (D9)      | —                             | flags 8 / 12, **0 / 21 false positives** |

Corollaries that constrain any fix:

- **For `wc <= 2` the promotion happens in section 7, not gate 6.** `rule_short_line` (`text_util.py:1356-1413`) always returns, so nothing below it — including all six section-9 gates and the section-10 band routing — is reachable for 1–2 token lines. Its final branch returns `Clear` when `valid_word_ratio >= 1.0`.
- **`wc == 3` is a different regime.** `SHORT_PPL_CAP` covers `wc <= 2` only, so 3-token lines already reach the perplexity routes with raw perplexity, `damaged` becomes live, and sections 8–10 are reachable. That, not "shorter is riskier" as a law, explains the 3.6 / 5.1 / 6.8 : 1 gradient in the grading — consistent with §4's flat error rates once borderline lines are included.
- **`check_rescues()` cannot supply the `Noisy` floor.** All three of its floors — `_trailing_fill_rescued()`, `is_forgiven_headline()`, `is_clean_reference()` — evaluate `False` on all 11 lines tested, so it returns `Trash` for every one, `malakofauna` and `1 ks` included. Routing gate 6 through it makes the symmetry argument literally true and changes **nothing** on the disputed population.

**7. 🆕 Measured behaviour of the candidate cascade.**
Adding an initial-geminate test, a triple-character test and a character-variety test (distinct chars / length ≤ 0.45 at length ≥ 6) to the two existing run regexes, applied only to alphabetic tokens of length ≥ 4 not in `_NEUTRAL_LEXICON` / `SHORT_EXCEPTION_TOKENS` / `SHORT_VALID_WORDS`, and vetoed by `is_structured_line()` / `is_domain_notation()` / `has_cz_diacs()`:

| set                             | n  | flagged                                                                                                                              |
|---------------------------------|----|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| thread-reported garbage         | 12 | **8** — adds `vansasaasasa`, `rragment`, `NINNNIC`, `IDIDIDIDIDIDUOID` to the four the run tests reach                               |
| real vocabulary + notation      | 21 | **0** — incl. `Canis familiaris`, `Phalanx proximalis`, `Maxilla+dentes`, `Lokalisace: MM-III`                                       |
| all committed positive fixtures | 33 | **0** — `CLEAR`, `NOISY`, `ROT_FALSE_POSITIVE_GUARDS`, `HEADLINE_NUMBERED`, `SHORT_EXCEPTIONS`, `NOTATION_SHORT`, `VOCABULARY_SHORT` |
| `TRASH_GARBAGE` fixtures        | 5  | 1                                                                                                                                    |

Against exactly the lines @david-spacil printed in his §7 grading tables: **8 of 15** labelled *wrongly lifted* are flagged, **0 of 25** labelled *correctly lifted*, **0 of 4** borderline. Without the length/lexicon guard the geminate test false-positives on `mm`; the guard removes it. Residue unreached: `edelite`, `vfetennl k.`, `Slaot-o hiezazzt` — phonotactically legal, and the genuine lexicon cases.

> ⚠️ **Provenance and the harness caveat.** §5–§7 were measured in an environment without `pandas`, so `tools/recategorize_from_csv._rescore_row` could not be used; the signals were assembled from `text_util` directly, mirroring `classify_TEXT.score_line`'s order including the trust tiers and `SHORT_PPL_CAP`. **Every figure in §5–§7 must be re-run through the production re-scorer before it is quoted publicly.** That is exactly the harness-divergence bug this thread has already hit twice (`test_rotation_regression.py`, and `_categ`'s hardcoded `ces_Latn`), and a third instance is still live in the tree — see D13. The grading-table numbers above are also **not an error rate**: those are the examples the contributor chose to print, not a random sample of the 508.

## Algorithm sketch — where a short line is decided now

1. **Section 1** — three perplexity routes. Notation is exempt from `rule_extreme_ppl` and `rule_absolute_ppl`; `rule_hard_sweep` still applies when FastText independently failed to place the line. At `wc <= 2` none of the three can fire at all, because `SHORT_PPL_CAP` (850) sits below every threshold.
2. **Sections 2–4** — inverted scan, all-caps, garbage density. Unchanged. Notation is not immune here: `Reg.Bez.Aussig.` sits at `rot_ratio` 0.583 against a 0.65 threshold, `II/C` at `garbage_density` 0.25 against 0.35.
3. **Gate 6 `rule_short_garbage`** (`text_util.py:1337-1345`) — skipped when `forgiven`, `structured`, or `is_domain_notation()`. Convicts on shape alone and returns `Trash` unconditionally, without consulting `check_rescues()`. **This is the only rule that convicts the disputed population**; with PR #48's gate it convicts none of it (§6).
4. **Gate 7 `rule_short_line`** (`text_util.py:1356-1413`) — total for `wc <= 2`; every branch returns, so sections 8–10 and `check_rescues()` are unreachable for that population. Its `damage` term already reads the discriminating witness and discards it via `structurally_clean` (§5); its final branch returns `Clear` on `valid_word_ratio >= 1.0`, with `categorize_line` clamping the stored score *up* to `CATEG_NOISY_SCORE_MAX` (0.80) — `II/C` goes from a raw 0.4792 to a pinned 0.80.

Step 4 is where D9, D10 and D11 all belong, and it is the same "promotes to the top category, not a reviewable middle state" objection raised against the contributor's patch. It applies to notation lifted by D6 verbatim and should be stated rather than buried.

## A decision-quality checklist for this class of change

Derived from what actually went wrong in this thread, and worth applying to D9/D10 before they ship:

1. **Is the headline metric monotone by construction?** "367,208 lifted / 0 demoted" was — removing an unconditional `return Trash` cannot demote anything. A direction that was never in question is not evidence.
2. **Does the harness compute what production computes?** Three harnesses in this repo have fed `categorize_line` a value the pipeline never produces. Two are fixed; one is still live (D13).
3. **Can the test suite see the change?** The golden `short_garbage` case is set up so that the gate under review cannot alter its outcome.
4. **Does the new feature reach a rule?** `apply_page_perplexity_blend()` and `rule_domain_notation` both shipped in `8fadeb9` unable to change any outcome, for two different reasons.
5. **Is the analogy load-bearing or decorative?** The "seventh sibling of six gated rules" symmetry did not survive reading `50472fb`'s diff.
6. **Is the signal absent, or present and discarded?** Research §5 is the case in point — three rounds concluded a lexicon was required while the discriminating witness sat two lines above the conjunct that suppressed it.
