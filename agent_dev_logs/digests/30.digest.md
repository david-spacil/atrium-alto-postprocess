## 🎯 Goal & Core Constraint

The issue was opened as "the 2010+ documents are categorised badly", and the cause turned out to be one rule. `rule_short_garbage` (`text_util.py:1337-1345`, gate 6 of `determine_category`) returns `Trash` **unconditionally** for any 1–3 token line without Czech diacritics, weak language ID and any weirdness. It is the largest single Trash producer in the corpus — 6,333 firings, **28.4% of all Trash lines**, 62.5% on the two documents @DanaKriv flagged — and it concentrates on post-2000 documents precisely because those are tabular: one value per cell is exactly its firing profile.

The constraint, as it stood for most of this thread, was that nothing in the feature set separates the two populations the rule acts on. **Finding 1 below revises that**, and it is the main change since the last digest.

## 🏆 Headline Findings & Conclusions

**1. The pipeline already computes a signal that separates the disputed classes — and then discards it.**
This supersedes the "no character-level rule separates them; a lexicon is required" conclusion carried by the previous digest and by PR #48's own *Known limitation* section. `detect_fused_words()` (`text_util.py:757`), built on `_RE_FUSED_VOWEL_RUN` (3+ vowel run) and `_RE_FUSED_CONSONANT_RUN` (5+ consonant run), returns **1** for `oueussd`, `sektlll`, `cuxoaid` and `Tthts I`, and **0** for `malakofauna`, `diapozitiv`, `Occipitale`, `Equus caballus`, `Kaaden`, `Pinii`, `kontext`, `Uniocrassus`, `Ossa tarsi`, `radius prox.sin.`, `1 ks` and `II/C`.

Section 7 (`rule_short_line`) **already reads it**, inside its `damage` term:

```python
or ((gibberish_present or detect_fused_words(text_source) > 0) and not structurally_clean)
```

with `structurally_clean = valid_word_ratio >= 1.0`. But `valid_word_ratio` is a **shape-only** heuristic — `compute_valid_ratio()` (`text_util.py:2253`) counts a token valid on length ≥ 3, ≥ 70% alphabetic characters, no strange character and no mid-word uppercase — so it is **1.0 for every line in this population**, `oueussd` included. The one witness that discriminates is suppressed by the one signal that cannot. The limitation is therefore a **suppression bug in the short-line path**, not an absence of signal.

**2. On this population the PR's gate is equivalent to deleting the rule.**
`_has_strong_garbage_evidence()` (`text_util.py:1646-1678`) returned **False on 16 of 16** disputed lines. With `valid_word_ratio` 1.0, `weird_ratio ≈ 0.35`, gibberish 0 and density 0.0, its only live clause is `lang_score <= 0.20 and orig_lang_score <= 0.50` — i.e. `orig_lang <= 0.40` once `TRUST_TIER_UNKNOWN` (0.50) has scaled it. Measured through the real `determine_category`:

| variant                       | real vocabulary → `Trash`     | garbage → not-`Trash`                                |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| baseline (`test` @ `4139e93`) | 8 / 9 — the issue's complaint | 0 / 7                                                |
| + PR #48's gate               | 0 / 9                         | **7 / 7, all reaching `Clear`**                      |
| candidate witness cascade     | —                             | flags 8 / 12, **0 false positives on 21 real lines** |

So the gate is a stronger change than "adding a second witness" suggests: on the lines under dispute there is no second witness to consult, and the rule simply stops convicting.

**3. The trade is real and, this round, deliberately accepted.**
PR #48 lifts 367,208 lines, demotes none, at a weighted 76.5% right / 9.6% borderline / 13.9% wrong (484 lines scored blind by a human annotator, key held aside, design-weighted with decade strata) — a 5.5 : 1 ratio. @DanaKriv's separate contribution was a review of both collections fully recomputed with the change, and her verdict was in favour of adopting it. Roughly **26,000 garbage lines reach `Clear`**, the top category rather than a reviewable middle state. The decision is to ship that and narrow it in a follow-up rather than hold the improvement; the `strict=True` xfail on `oueussd` is the tracking device, and it becomes `XPASS(strict)` — deliberately red — the moment the follow-up lands.

**4. Against the graded examples, the candidate witness separates cleanly — as a hypothesis, not a result.**
Run over exactly the lines @david-spacil printed in his §7 grading tables: **8 of the 15** he labelled *wrongly lifted* are flagged (`oueussd`, `vansasaasasa`, `sektlll`, `rragment`, `NINNNIC`, `cuxoaid`, `Tthts I`, `IDIDIDIDIDIDUOID`), **0 of the 25** he labelled *correctly lifted*, and **0 of the 4** borderline. Also 0 false positives across all 33 committed positive fixtures (`CLEAR`, `NOISY`, `ROT_FALSE_POSITIVE_GUARDS`, `HEADLINE_NUMBERED`, `SHORT_EXCEPTIONS`, `NOTATION_SHORT`, `VOCABULARY_SHORT`).

These are the examples he chose to print, not a random sample, so this is a feasibility signal and nothing more. If that recall held on the real changed population the wrong-lift share would fall from 13.9% toward 6–7% at no cost to the correct lifts. **That must be measured on the 508 annotated lines before it is claimed.** A residue — `edelite`, `vfetennl k.`, `Slaot-o hiezazzt` — is phonotactically legal and genuinely needs a lexicon; the earlier conclusion survives for that subset.

**5. `check_rescues()` cannot supply the reviewable middle state.**
Routing gate 6 through it would make the PR's symmetry argument literally true, but it changes nothing here: all three of its floors — `_trailing_fill_rescued()`, `is_forgiven_headline()`, `is_clean_reference()` — evaluate `False` on all 11 lines tested, so it returns `Trash` for every one, `malakofauna` and `1 ks` included. A `Noisy` floor for `wc <= 2` has to be implemented explicitly. Note also that for 1–2 token lines the promotion to `Clear` happens in **section 7**, which always returns, and not in gate 6 — so that is where both the witness and any floor belong.

**6. The PR's red check is inherited from its base and is already fixed on `test`.**
The only failing check is `Docker Build & Publish / docker-build-smoke (api)` ([run 34233501343](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-alto-postprocess/actions/runs/34233501343)). The container dies at import: `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'atrium_document'`, because the `api` stage's `ENTRYPOINT ["python", "service/text_api.py"]` puts `/app/service` on `sys.path[0]` and not `/app`, and on the PR's base the three repo-root imports sit *above* the bootstrap. `4139e93` fixed exactly that and added `tests/test_service_entrypoint.py`. **Merging `test` into the PR branch turns the check green with no behavioural risk** — `git merge-tree` reports no conflict, and the merge cannot move a category because the algorithm files are byte-identical. Separately, the `API Meta-Contract` workflow is green on every recent run and was never the problem; the 2026-09-06 nightly `slow-tests` failure (unquoted `PRE_INSTALL_TORCH` reaching the shell as redirections) was fixed by `94cf2f1` and is already in the PR's base.

**7. The probe that would have caught that regression never runs on pushes — and the suite is blind to the change under review.**
`docker-build-smoke` is gated `if: github.event_name == 'pull_request'` in the hub reusable (`ufal/atrium-project/.github/workflows/docker-tool.reusable.yml:270`). The `api` stage arrived on a push (`1bd64d8`), so nothing ever started the image until a fork PR did; and on the push of the fix `4139e93` the job was **skipped**, so the fix is published but still unproven by an actual container start. Three more gaps found alongside:

- `tests/fixtures/pipeline_characterization.json`'s `short_garbage` edge case is set up with `gibberish_present=True` **and** `valid_word_ratio=0.0` (`tests/test_pipeline_parity.py:194-208`), either of which alone short-circuits `_has_strong_garbage_evidence()` to `True`. The golden file therefore **cannot see** PR #48's change, which is why the fast lane stayed green while four fixtures flipped. `_fire("rule_short_garbage")` also sits *above* the gate, so rule-coverage telemetry cannot distinguish "fired" from "convicted".
- `tests/test_smoke.py:24-73` is a **third** hand-rolled scoring harness, still carrying the bug fixed twice already: it feeds `categorize_line` the `LANG_SCORE_REMAP` cap instead of `trust_lang_score`, and omits `orig_lang_score` and `garbage_density` entirely, leaving them at the `1.0` / `0.0` defaults that silently disable `rule_hard_sweep`, `rule_extreme_ppl` and the density branch of `_has_strong_garbage_evidence`.
- `tests/test_rotation_regression.py:83` sweeps 7 constants across their `SEARCH_SPACE` bounds only to prove clean Czech is never demoted. There is no counterpart asserting garbage stays `Trash`, so a rule that is inert at default configuration looks identical to a correct one.

**8. The PR's red check is now fixed at the source, not just diagnosable.**
`docker-build-smoke` was gated `if: github.event_name == 'pull_request'` in the hub reusable, which is why the `api` stage could arrive on a push, never be started, and then fail on an unrelated contributor's PR — and why the fix, also pushed, shipped unproven. It now runs on pushes to `test` and on `v*` tags as well. Its two budgets were also inverted against the convention they test: the healthy wait expired at 240s, 30s **before** Docker can declare a container unhealthy (`--start-period=180s` + 3 × `--interval=30s` = 270s), so a genuinely broken container was reported as an ambiguous timeout; and `docker stop --time 15` sent SIGKILL five seconds before uvicorn's own `GRACEFUL_SHUTDOWN_S=20` deadline, so a service that actually used its drain window would have failed the probe for behaving correctly — it passed only because the probe sends no requests, which is the case it is not testing. Both are now derived from the `HEALTHCHECK` convention and documented as such. ⚠️ These reach the five consumer repos only when the `v1` tag moves; it currently points at `ad1a788`.

**9. 🆕 The previous digest's fix was in the wrong place, and it would have failed silently.**
Finding 1 said the witness belongs in section 7's `damage` term, and the plan recorded that landing it there would turn the `oueussd` xfail into `XPASS(strict)` — the signal that the debt is paid. Traced on a patched copy of the module, it does not:

| line      | baseline | + evidence gate | + gate and the section-7 fix |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|------------------------------|
| `oueussd` | `Trash`  | `Clear`         | **`Noisy`**                  |
| `sektlll` | `Trash`  | `Clear`         | **`Noisy`**                  |

Section 7's `damage` branch returns `Noisy` (`text_util.py:1398-1399`); the test asserts `== "Trash"`. So the test stays `xfail` forever, the tracking device never fires, and the visible improvement (`Clear` → `Noisy`) would have been mistaken for the debt being cleared. **The witness has to reach gate 6, the only site in this path that returns `Trash`.**

**10. 🆕 The "run-only form of `detect_fused_words`" would have demoted clean Czech.**
It is two regexes, and `_RE_FUSED_CONSONANT_RUN` (5+ consonants) matches `vrstva`, `vrstvy`, `vrstvou`, `vrstvami`, `ctvrtek`, `ctvrt`, `zmrzl`, `scvrkl` — ordinary diacritic-free Czech. `vrstva` ("layer") is the commonest noun in archaeological field documentation, and `vrstva 3` satisfies every condition of gate 6 at production signals, so a witness carrying that clause would send it to `Trash`. That is a demotion of clean text: the one outcome this thread exists to prevent. Only the **vowel** run is safe, and nothing is lost — `sektlll` is reached by a triple-character test and `Tthts I` by a geminate test instead.

The corrected predicate, `_has_shape_garbage_evidence()`, is now in the tree behind `SHORT_GARBAGE_WITNESS_ENABLE` (default **false**, no call site yet): **9/9** on the thread's reachable garbage, **0/42** on vocabulary, notation, toponyms, names and units, **0/37** on every committed positive fixture row. Its two-line wiring into gate 6 is the only piece that must wait for the PR, because it lands inside the PR's own hunk.

**11. 🆕 Finding 7's blind spots are closed, and two more were found in the ablation tooling.**
The suite went 814 → 891 passing. The golden file now carries three edge cases that can actually see a gate on this rule (they go red when the PR merges — that is the artifact); `_has_strong_garbage_evidence()` being `False` on this whole population is pinned on production vectors instead of measured in a harness; `test_smoke.py`'s third hand-rolled scorer is retired onto `_rescore_row`; and the swept-bounds robustness gate has a Trash-side counterpart. Separately: four entries in **both** ablation drivers were still spelled `penalty_*` from before those gates were renamed, so `DISABLED_RULES` matched nothing on them and every ablation report since carried four rows of pure zero that read as "prune" for a rule that had never been disabled — and eleven real rules were missing outright, so the study covered 11 of 22. Both lists are now the registry, and a test keeps them there.

## 🏁 Final Bottom Line

The direction was right, it is cheaper than it looked, and this round it stopped being an argument and became measurements. What the last digest got right survives: the blocking claim — *no character-level rule separates `oueussd` from `malakofauna`* — is false as stated, the separating signal is already computed, and the open problem is much smaller than "acquire a lexicon".

What the last digest got **wrong** is worth stating plainly, because both errors were the kind that pass review. The fix was placed where it cannot return `Trash`, so it would have looked like progress while the device tracking the debt stayed silent (finding 9). And it was specified to reuse a regex that matches `vrstva` — the commonest noun in this corpus — so it would have traded one demotion problem for another (finding 10). Neither was visible from reading the code; both took running it.

What is settled: perplexity cannot arbitrate this population; notation is recoverable by shape; `check_rescues()` cannot provide a `Noisy` floor; section 7, not gate 6, is where 1–2 token lines are decided — **and that is precisely why the witness does not belong there**.

What is not settled is the one thing no amount of code reading fixes. The corrected predicate's 9/9-vs-0/42 separation is a smoke test, not a measurement: those are the lines this thread happened to print. The in-tree corpus is **15 lines, 5 of them 1–3 tokens**, so nothing about this change can be validated against it. The flag stays `false` until it is measured on the 508, and that is why the annotation files are the top ask below rather than a nice-to-have.

PR #48 is taken as-is on its measured 5.5 : 1 trade, with ~26,000 garbage lines reaching `Clear` in the interim. The tree is now ready for it: nothing landed this round can conflict with its two hunks, the suite can see what it does, and the narrowing is a two-line change waiting on the merge.

**Two things still have to be requested rather than derived**: the **508-line and 1,567-line annotation files**, which exist only as @david-spacil's own files and are the entire basis for measuring any of this, and the `30.audit.md` / `30.strategy.md` attachments, which were never in the tree.
